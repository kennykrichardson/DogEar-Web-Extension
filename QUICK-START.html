DogEar • Quick start guide

Keep the thought.
Not just the tab.

Save the passage that matters, add your perspective, and return to it with its original source still attached. This guide walks you through DogEar's quick-capture flow.

01 / Capture from any page

Save a selection in seconds.

1

Select the passage you want to keep on a webpage.

2

Right-click the highlighted text. Choose Save selection to DogEar from the context menu.

3

The DogEar popup opens with your selected passage and page details ready to review.

Look for the red outline. It marks the exact DogEar command in the browser's right-click menu.
02 / Review and save

Your thought, attached to its source.

DogEar opens a compact capture popup. The selected quote and page title are prefilled so you can add context before saving.

1

Add a note explaining why the passage matters or what you want to remember.

2

Add tags separated by commas, then choose a marker color if useful.

3

Select “Save to DogEar”. Your annotation is stored locally in the browser.

03 / Make it yours

Keep your library useful.

A good capture is more than a quote. Give it enough context that future-you knows why it mattered.

Notes and tags

Add your own interpretation and searchable labels to each saved passage.

Marker colors

Use the available color markers to visually distinguish annotations.

Full library

Choose “Open your full library” in the popup to browse and manage saved annotations in the workspace.

Local by design

Your saved annotations are stored locally. The popup describes optional access as limited to the current site, not the whole browser.

About the workspace screenshots: the supplied images show the right-click menu and the two halves of the popup. No workspace screenshot was included, so this guide explains how to open the library without inventing a screenshot of a screen you have not supplied.
04 / Handy reminders

Small details. Better habits.

Capture only what matters

Short, meaningful passages are easier to revisit than entire pages copied without context.

Write for your future self

A one-sentence note can explain why a quote mattered weeks later.

Use site access intentionally

Enable optional highlighting access only on sites where you want DogEar to bring saved highlights back.

Keep a backup

Use DogEar's backup/export features in the full library when you want an extra copy of your notes.
