Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of how DogEar handles information when you save and organize notes from the web.

Last updated: November 9, 2026

In short: DogEar is designed as a local-first browser extension. It stores the annotations and preferences you create in your browser. DogEar does not require an account or a developer-operated cloud service to provide its core annotation features.

1. About DogEar

DogEar helps you save selected passages from webpages, add personal notes, organize saved items, and return to them later. This policy describes the information handled by the extension and the purposes for which it is used.

2. Information you choose to save

When you use DogEar to create an annotation, the extension may store information associated with that item, such as:

  • The webpage text or passage you select.
  • The source page's URL and title.
  • Notes you write, and tags or collections you create.
  • Annotation status and organizational details, such as pinned or archived state.
  • Extension preferences and settings.

DogEar handles webpage content when you explicitly use its saving or annotation features. It is not designed to collect a general browsing history.

3. How information is used

The information described above is used to provide DogEar's features, including saving, displaying, editing, searching, organizing, re-highlighting, backing up, and exporting your annotations. It is not used for advertising, user profiling, or purposes unrelated to DogEar's annotation functionality.

4. Storage and transmission

DogEar stores annotation data and preferences locally using browser extension storage. The extension is designed to operate without a developer-operated backend, account system, or cloud synchronization service. DogEar does not transmit saved annotations to a server operated by the developer.

Your browser and its extension storage controls govern the local data. Removing the extension or clearing its stored data may delete annotations. Use DogEar's export or backup features, where available, to keep a separate copy.

5. Sharing and sale of information

DogEar does not sell your saved annotations or share them with third parties for advertising. The extension does not intentionally send saved annotation content to third parties.

6. Permissions

DogEar requests browser permissions to support its core features:

  • activeTab: Allows the extension to interact with the active tab after a user action, such as capturing a selected passage.
  • storage: Persists annotations and extension preferences in browser extension storage.
  • contextMenus: Adds a browser context-menu action so you can save selected text from a webpage.
  • scripting: Supports webpage interaction required for annotation capture and re-highlighting saved passages.

Permissions are used to provide DogEar's stated functionality, not for advertising or unrelated tracking.

7. Your choices and control

You can manage your saved information using DogEar's available controls, including editing, organizing, archiving, exporting, and deleting annotations. You can also manage or remove extension data through your browser's extension settings and storage controls.

8. Children's privacy

DogEar is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not specifically directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated service.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if DogEar's features or data practices change. The revised version will be published at this same URL, with the updated date shown at the top of the page.
